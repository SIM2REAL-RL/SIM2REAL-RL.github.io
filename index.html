03 / METHOD OVERVIEWCFA + APC

THE METHOD.

Sim2Real-RL jointly trains a visual actor and a privileged critic in randomized simulation. CFA localizes interaction-relevant regions and refines spatial attention; APC guides visual representation learning with privileged task states and transition-wise advantages. At deployment, the actor uses RGB observations and proprioception, without real-world policy fine-tuning.

PRIVILEGED SUPERVISION
TRAINING ONLY ↗
WHERE TO LOOK

Coarse regions.
Fine attention.

Localize interaction-relevant regions containing the end effector and task objects. Then refine spatial attention within those regions to extract control-relevant features.

At deployment, both stages operate on RGB observations without simulator-provided annotations.

WHAT TO LEARN

Visual features.
Privileged guidance.

Advantage-aware Privileged Contrastive Learning aligns visual and privileged-state representations from the same transition. Transition-wise advantages weight the contrastive objective.

Privileged-state encoding and advantage weights are used only during simulation training.

Zero-shot here means no real-world policy fine-tuning; the setup still includes approximate camera alignment.
