01 / THE IDEA

Learn what matters.
Transfer what works.

Sim2Real-RL trains visual manipulation policies end-to-end in randomized simulation and deploys them directly on a real robot without policy fine-tuning. An asymmetric actor–critic uses privileged task states for value estimation, while the actor learns from RGB observations and proprioception.

Coarse-to-Fine Attention (CFA) first localizes interaction-relevant regions and then refines spatial attention within them. Advantage-aware Privileged Contrastive Learning (APC) uses privileged states and transition-wise advantages to guide visual representation learning during training.

One learning framework, with a separately trained policy for each task.

Where to look
APC

Advantage-aware Privileged Contrastive Learning

Align visual features with privileged task-state representations during simulation training. Transition-wise advantages weight the contrastive objective, giving more emphasis to favorable transitions.

What to learn

At deployment: the actor uses RGB observations and proprioception. Simulator annotations, privileged states, and real-world policy fine-tuning are not required.
